Access Management

A perfect fit for businesses that want easy, hassle-free access control.

Our access management tool lets you easily control visibility and permissions across your system. You can show, hide, or set read-only access for modules, users, or business units—covering fields, models, menus, records, buttons, tabs, views, actions, reports, and even chatter. Everything can be managed from one centralized place, without the need for complex configurations or technical know-how.

Does this app work well with Odoo enterprise and community?

Yes, the app works perfectly well with Odoo enterprise (On-premise and Odoo.SH) as well as community. Since, Odoo Online (Cloud) does not allow installation of third-party apps, this app cannot be installed on Odoo Online.

I need an extra feature in this app. How can I request it?

You can contact us to request a customization in the app.

Do I get free upgrades and support if I purchase the app?

If you purchase our app for Odoo Version 12 and prior, we will provide you access to all the versions up to 12 depending on their availability. Starting version 13, every version of the app will be sold separately. We offer free support for a duration of 90 days from the date of purchase. Kindly read our support policy to know the details.
